# Product scope: roadmap and non-goals

The product is deliberately "one Account, one shared Profile, several always-public
Portfolios of Projects, published at `/cv/{id}`." The lists below are recorded so
they are not re-proposed without a reason to revisit.

## Roadmap (wanted, not now)

- PDF / CV export
- Multiple page themes
- Password-reset flow and signup email verification (see ADR-0002)

## Out of scope (not planned)

- Custom domains
- Visitor analytics
- A contact form that sends email
- Team / organization accounts
- Internationalisation
